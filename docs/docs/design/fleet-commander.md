# Fleet Commander

The Fleet Commander is your persistent character — the admiral who leads your fleet across the campaign. Inspired by BFG Armada's admiral system, the commander gains experience, unlocks abilities, and shapes the identity of your entire fleet.

## Commander Profile

Every commander has:

| Attribute | Description |
|-----------|-------------|
| **Name** | Player-chosen (e.g., "Admiral Korvus") |
| **Title** | Earned through progression (e.g., "Lord Admiral", "Warmaster") |
| **Faction** | Imperial Navy, Chaos Fleet, etc. |
| **Level** | 1-10, gained through XP |
| **XP** | Earned from battles (victories, objectives, kills) |
| **Flagship** | The ship the commander is aboard — gains access to active abilities |
| **Active Ability Slots** | Unlocked at levels 1, 3, 5, 7 (max 4) |
| **Passive Skill Slots** | Unlocked at levels 2, 4, 6, 8 (max 4) |
| **Traits** | Earned from narrative events, permanent modifiers |
| **Fleet Capacity** | Max points value of your fleet, increases with level |

## Active Abilities

Active abilities are **used from the flagship only** during battle. They have **cooldowns** (measured in turns) and sometimes **limited charges** per battle.

### Universal Active Abilities

Any faction's commander can choose from these:

#### Micro Warp Jump
- **Effect**: Teleport the flagship to any position within an allied ship's sensor range
- **Cooldown**: 5 turns
- **Charges**: 2 per battle (1 for battleships)
- **Preparation**: Takes 1 turn to prepare — the jump executes next turn
- **Counter**: Can be interrupted by boarding actions during preparation
- **Use case**: Emergency repositioning, flanking, escaping a losing engagement

#### Emergency Repairs
- **Effect**: Restore D3 hull points to the flagship. Extinguish all fires. Repair temporary critical damage.
- **Cooldown**: 4 turns
- **Charges**: 2 per battle
- **Use case**: Keeping the flagship alive when under heavy fire

#### Call to Arms (Rally)
- **Effect**: All friendly ships within 40 GU of the flagship restore 30 morale. Cancels mutiny on affected ships.
- **Cooldown**: 5 turns
- **Charges**: 3 per battle
- **Use case**: Preventing a fleet rout when morale is collapsing

#### Augur Probe
- **Effect**: Deploy a sensor probe at a target location. The probe provides Level 3 (Identified) detection in a 30 GU radius for 3 turns.
- **Cooldown**: 3 turns
- **Charges**: 3 per battle
- **Use case**: Scouting, revealing Running Silent ships, enabling Micro Warp Jumps to distant positions

#### Concentrated Fire
- **Effect**: All friendly ships within 30 GU gain +1 gunnery column shift against a designated target for 2 turns.
- **Cooldown**: 5 turns
- **Charges**: 2 per battle
- **Use case**: Focus-firing a priority target, burning down a battleship

### Faction-Specific Active Abilities

#### Imperial Navy
- **Torpedo Barrage**: All torpedo-armed ships in range immediately fire a bonus torpedo salvo (does not consume stock). Cooldown: 6 turns, 1 charge.
- **Boarding Assault**: The flagship launches a powerful boarding action at range 15 GU (instead of the normal close range). 3 assault actions. Cooldown: 5 turns, 2 charges.

#### Chaos Fleet
- **Warp Rift**: Open a warp rift at a target point. All ships within 15 GU take D3 hull damage and lose 20 morale. Cooldown: 6 turns, 1 charge.
- **Mark of Chaos**: The flagship gains +2 lance strength and immunity to morale loss for 3 turns. Cooldown: 7 turns, 1 charge.

## Passive Skills

Passive skills provide **permanent fleet-wide bonuses** as long as the commander is alive. They are always active — no cooldowns or charges.

### Universal Passive Skills

| Skill | Effect |
|-------|--------|
| **Short-Burn Torpedoes** | All torpedoes travel 50% faster |
| **Shield Harmonics** | Fleet shield regeneration +1 per End Phase |
| **Master Gunner** | All weapon batteries gain +1 firepower at close range |
| **Veteran Crews** | Fleet-wide morale loss reduced by 25% |
| **Swift Maneuvers** | All ships gain +5 speed |
| **Reinforced Bulkheads** | Hull Breach critical hits deal no extra damage |
| **Sensor Mastery** | Fleet sensor range increased by 20 GU |
| **Iron Discipline** | Ships cannot mutiny while within 40 GU of the flagship |

### Faction-Specific Passive Skills

#### Imperial Navy
| Skill | Effect |
|-------|--------|
| **Prow of the Emperor** | All prow armor values +1 |
| **Reload Drills** | Torpedo reload takes 1 fewer turn |
| **Boarding Expertise** | All boarding actions gain +1 assault action |

#### Chaos Fleet
| Skill | Effect |
|-------|--------|
| **Lance Mastery** | All lances hit on 3+ instead of 4+ |
| **Dark Blessings** | Ships regenerate 1 hull per End Phase while below 50% hull |
| **Speed of Chaos** | All Ahead Full grants +3D6 speed instead of +2D6 |

## Commander Traits

Traits are **permanent modifiers** earned through narrative events during the campaign. They cannot be chosen freely — they are awarded based on your actions and the story.

| Trait | How Earned | Effect |
|-------|-----------|--------|
| **Veteran of Cadia** | Survive a story mission at Cadia | Fleet morale +10 baseline |
| **Reckless** | Win 3 battles with under 25% fleet remaining | +5 speed, -10 morale baseline |
| **Cautious Strategist** | Win 5 battles without losing a ship | Brace for Impact stance is 30% more effective |
| **Torpedo Ace** | Destroy 10 ships with torpedoes across campaign | Torpedoes gain +1 strength |
| **The Butcher** | Destroy 3 ships via boarding in a single battle | Boarding actions gain +2 assault actions, morale penalty in friendly ports |
| **Warp-Touched** | Survive a warp storm event | Micro Warp Jump gains +1 charge, -5 morale baseline |

## The Flagship

The **flagship** is the ship your commander is physically aboard. It has special significance:

- **Only the flagship can use active abilities**
- **Leadership bonus**: +1 to the flagship's Leadership value
- **Morale anchor**: Nearby ships benefit from Iron Discipline (if passive skill is taken)
- **Loss condition**: If the flagship is destroyed in battle, the commander survives (usually) but all remaining ships must make morale checks. If the commander dies (rare, catastrophic event), the campaign takes a severe hit.
- **You can change your flagship** between battles, but not during them

## Multiplayer Commander Builds

In multiplayer, commanders use a simplified build system:

- **Fixed level** (agreed upon by players, typically level 5 or 7)
- **2 active ability slots** (choose from universal + faction pool)
- **2 passive skill slots** (choose from universal + faction pool)
- **No traits** (multiplayer balance — traits are campaign-only)
- **Fleet points budget** (agreed upon, typically 1000-1500)

This creates a "commander build" metagame where players must choose synergistic ability and skill combinations to complement their fleet composition.
